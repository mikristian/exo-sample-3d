01 / THE IDEA

Less rebuilding.
More creating.

Today, designers often have to model everyday garment hardware repeatedly. A trusted community library could make simple, useful components available directly inside the software—complete with sensible dimensions, materials and provenance.

We're inviting modelers, designers and technical artists to share original reusable 3D assets. Contributions will need licensing and technical review before being accepted or embedded in the desktop app.

03 / QUALITY MAKES IT REUSABLE

Design it once.
Make it dependable.

Good asset donations are more than good-looking renders. The best submissions can be inspected, scaled and integrated without guessing at their dimensions or usage rights.

01 / MODEL FILES

Editable and portable

Preferred: glTF/GLB for preview and Blender source (.blend) where available. OBJ/MTL is useful for geometry exchange. Keep external textures and dependencies clearly listed.

02 / REAL SCALE

Millimeters and measurements

Provide measured width, height, thickness, attachment spacing and intended orientation. Use consistent origin and units; a screenshot alone cannot verify physical dimensions.

03 / CLEAN MESH

Optimized geometry

Clean normals, sensible polygon counts, separated moving parts where needed, no hidden geometry and no unexpected modifiers or linked assets. Include UVs when appropriate.

04 / MATERIALS

Usable surface data

Include simple named materials and optional PBR textures. Keep metalness, roughness and color maps recognizable and permission-cleared.

05 / PROVENANCE

Know who made it

Include creator or studio credit, asset name, original authoring tool, source URL if public and a short explanation of how measurements were obtained.

06 / LICENSE

Rights that travel

CC0 is the preferred option for redistributable built-in assets. Other terms require individual review. No brand logos, paid library models, client files or proprietary measurements without permission.

PROPOSED NATIVE LIBRARY PIPELINE

Donation → review → validation → asset library → future desktop integration

Being proposed does not mean accepted. Integration into ClothCraft releases depends on rights checks, geometry verification, conversion and maintainers' approval.
